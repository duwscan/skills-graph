- 8 to 15 categories that represent real hiring-market skill clusters

- Organize around actual hiring-market skill clusters, not academic classifications.
- Keep categories mutually distinct as much as possible.
- Prioritize business-usable taxonomy for job matching and skills graph enrichment.
